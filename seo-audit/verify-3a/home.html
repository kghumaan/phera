We collect every detail from your guests

The problem: You're chasing 300 guests across five WhatsApp groups for RSVPs, dietary needs, and +1 counts — while your uncle insists he already replied (he didn't).

Our solution: We reach out on your behalf — save-the-dates, RSVPs, dietary, event-by-event attendance. Auto follow-ups for non-responders, escalations only when it matters.

A wedding website that actually gets used

The problem: Your auntie visited your wedding site once, then WhatsApped you three times asking for the venue, the dress code, and whether the kids are invited to the sangeet.

Our solution: A beautiful, bespoke site with schedule, FAQ, registry, and PIN-gated event access. Design it yourself, let AI build it, or have our team craft it 1-on-1.

Travel, shuttles, and rooms — handled

The problem: You're updating a spreadsheet of flight numbers at midnight. Your uncle's flight lands at 4 AM and nobody knows who is picking him up.

Our solution: We collect travel plans from every guest, manage hotel blocks and room assignments, optimize shuttle routes, and send pickup reminders. Nobody gets stranded at the airport.

We answer every guest question, 24/7

The problem: Your auntie is WhatsApping you at 2 AM about the dress code. Your cousin's fiancé wants to know if he needs a visa. You should be sleeping.

Our solution: Concierge is trained on your wedding data — venue, dates, local weather, nearby things to do. Guests get instant answers in English or Hindi: dress codes for each event, visa walkthroughs, airport pickups, restaurant picks near the hotel. You finally sleep.

Your vendor groups, finally organized

The problem: You're in eight WhatsApp groups — caterer, florist, decorator, DJ — playing telephone between them while your mom asks for 'a quick update' every hour.

Our solution: Add our Agent to your vendor groups. It summarizes threads, extracts action items, flags risks, and keeps every commitment on record. You'll never miss a detail again.

Your friends from abroad? We've got them.

The problem: Your college roommate from Brooklyn has no idea what a sangeet is. Your cousin's fiancé has never been to a baraat. They're flying in without a clue.

Our solution: Cultural briefings per event, dress-code guides, ceremony explainers — all delivered through WhatsApp before they board. They show up ready to enjoy, not Google.
