Indian weddings are beautiful chaos. Phera handles the guest logistics so you can focus on the celebration.

Phera is an AI wedding planner for Indian weddings — NRI-first. One agent runs the whole platform by chat (soon WhatsApp + voice): guest list, RSVPs, rooms, shuttles, vendors, and the midnight WhatsApps. The brand voice is warm, calm, and capable — premium without being stiff, cultural without being kitsch — pairing an editorial italic serif with a clean geometric sans, anchored by a single confident pink on a warm-paper canvas.

Logo & Logomark

The mark is a lotus-flame glyph — a lotus that reads as a flame, nodding to the sacred fire (agni) circled during the phera ritual. It locks up left of the “Phera” wordmark, set in the display serif. Logomark may stand alone as an app icon / avatar.

Typography

Display — Instrument Serif

Your desi wedding

Used at ≥ 2rem (h1–h3), typically italic. Weight 400 only. For hero, marquee, dialog titles, big numerals.

Body — Outfit

Light 300 · Regular 400 · Medium 500 · Semibold 600

Everything < 2rem: body, labels, buttons, captions. Weights 200–600. 14px (0.875rem) is the hard minimum for readable text.
