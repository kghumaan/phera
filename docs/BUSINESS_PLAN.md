# Phera — Business Plan

A complete reference for what Phera is, who it's for, what it sells, how it makes money, and how it goes to market. The strategic source-of-truth is `PIVOT-PLAN.md`; this doc consolidates that into a single business-context briefing.

---

## 1. One-Sentence Pitch

> **"Indian weddings are beautiful chaos — 300+ guests, 3-5 days of events, people flying in from everywhere. Phera handles the guest logistics so you can focus on the celebration."**

Positioning: **"Your Wedding Operations Team"** — *we coordinate your guests so you can focus on the celebration.*

We are not a wedding website builder. We are not a planner replacement. We are an operations service that delivers an outcome: every guest informed, every traveller coordinated, every question answered, every detail captured — without the couple chasing anyone on WhatsApp.

---

## 2. The Thesis

Built on Sequoia Capital's **"Services: The New Software"** framework (Julien Bek, March 5 2026).

**The two big ideas:**

1. **Intelligence vs. Judgment.** Intelligence work (data collection, outreach, scheduling, follow-ups) is rule-based and codifiable — AI now does it autonomously. Judgment work (taste, family dynamics, crisis management) stays human. We sell automated intelligence; we leave judgment to the couple and to local day-of coordinators.
2. **Outsourced work as the wedge.** When a task is already outsourced, the buyer has accepted external execution and budgeted for the outcome. Indian wedding guest coordination is already outsourced (planners spend 40-60 hours per destination wedding on it). That's the wedge.

**The shift in our model:** stop selling the tool, start selling the work. Couples don't want a wedding website — they want their 300 guests coordinated. So we deliver the outcome directly.

## 3. Target Market

### Primary — NRI couples (Non-Resident Indians)

Research revealed the strategic insight that defines the company: **NRIs are the primary market, not domestic India.**

**Market size:**
- 40,000–55,000 NRI weddings/year in India (<1% of volume, 3–5% of spend).
- Total segment: **$4–6 billion**.
- Avg NRI wedding budget: $60K–$200K. **US Indian Americans: $225K–$285K.**
- At $349–$599, Phera = **0.1–0.6% of budget**. Less than a single shuttle bus rental.

**Why NRIs first:**
- **Higher willingness to pay.** USD/GBP/AED earners conditioned to SaaS pricing. Won't flinch at $349–$599.
- **More pain.** 8–12 timezones from India-side logistics. Every 15-minute decision becomes a 3-day timezone delay.
- **More non-Indian guests.** College friends, work colleagues, partners from US/UK who've never been to India — they need cultural bridging that no platform provides.
- **Planner referral channel.** 40–60% of NRI weddings use planners (vs 20–30% domestic). Planners are the #1 GTM lever — they want to offload guest logistics.

**Source markets, by priority:** US (highest value), UK, Canada, UAE (high volume / more price-sensitive), Australia, Singapore.

### Secondary — Domestic Indian couples

₹9,999–₹29,999 INR pricing. We serve them, but we lead with NRIs.

### Tertiary — Wedding planners (B2B2C)

Planners managing 20–50 NRI destination weddings/year. Phera-for-Planners offers a multi-wedding Control Tower, client handoff, white-label option, and unlimited client weddings on a bulk/annual price (TBD).

## 4. Core Philosophy: Don't Take Away the Fun

Wedding planning has fun parts (vibe, design, aesthetic) and tedious parts (chasing 300 people on WhatsApp, tracking flights, coordinating shuttles). **Phera automates the tedious. The fun stays with the couple — unless they want us to do that too.**

### Three involvement levels

The couple chooses how hands-on they want to be:

1. **"I want to do it myself"** — Full DIY access to website, events, design. Phera runs guest logistics in the background.
2. **"Let AI help me"** — Build with AI (text or voice) creates the site from a conversation. Couple reviews. Phera handles the rest.
3. **"Work with an expert"** — 1-on-1 session with a Phera team member to nail the vision and event setup. Premium guidance. Phera coordinates.

This spectrum respects that wedding planning *is* fun for many couples. We remove the chaos, not the joy.

## 5. What We Sell — The Five Outcomes

We sell outcomes, not features. Every feature must roll up to one of these.

### Outcome 1 — Every guest is informed and accounted for

Phera proactively reaches out to every guest via WhatsApp: save-the-dates, RSVP requests, logistics collection, automatic follow-ups for non-responders. RSVPs happen on the couple's beautiful wedding website (the digital invitation for this generation). Everything after RSVP — travel, dietary, shuttle — happens via WhatsApp.

### Outcome 2 — All travel and transportation is coordinated

We track every guest's travel plans, optimize shuttle routes and timing, send reminders, handle changes. Nobody's stranded. The couple isn't chasing flight info.

### Outcome 3 — Guests are supported 24/7

Our WhatsApp Concierge answers guest questions instantly — schedule, venue, dress code, local recommendations — in their language, on their schedule.

### Outcome 4 — Friends from abroad? We've got them. *(most defensible feature)*

Non-Indian guests attending an Indian wedding in India get a WhatsApp-delivered cultural guide: visa info, what to wear (and what NOT to wear) at each event, ceremony explanations, airport-to-venue coordination, SIM card setup, food tips, and the reassurance that Indian families will be incredibly welcoming. **No competitor addresses this.** This is the feature NRI couples will share on Instagram.

### Outcome 5 — The couple sees everything, manages nothing

A real-time Control Tower dashboard shows guest response rates, travel status, shuttle capacity, and flags anything needing attention. The couple has visibility without doing the work. When something changes, they message Phera on WhatsApp and we handle the update.

### What we are NOT selling (yet)

- Vendor procurement / sourcing (Phase 2-3, after validation).
- Aesthetic / design services beyond the three involvement tiers.
- Day-of physical coordination — we recommend local day-of coordinators (see §10).
- Full wedding planning.

We are laser-focused on **guest logistics coordination** — the intelligence-heavy, already-outsourced work that planners spend 40–60 hours/wedding on.

## 7. The Hybrid WhatsApp Outreach Model

The product runs on Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API — the **official** API, not unofficial QR-linking shortcuts. Compliance and deliverability matter at scale.

### How outreach works

1. **Couple sends initial save-the-date** from their personal WhatsApp using `wa.me` deep links Phera generates. High trust, personal touch.
2. **All subsequent automation** comes from the Phera business number, branded "Priya & Rahul Wedding" with the couple's photo as profile picture.
3. Templates prompt replies → opens **free 24-hour service window** → all follow-up in that window is free.
4. WhatsApp **Flows** — multi-screen native forms inside WhatsApp — handle RSVP without leaving the app. **No competitor uses this.** Major differentiator.

### Cost & rules

- Marketing templates: ~₹0.86/msg, 50% delivery cold, 70–90% for opted-in audiences, 12–25% CTR realistic.
- Utility templates: ~₹0.14/msg, 95–99% delivery, exempt from frequency caps.
- Carousel templates (2–10 swipeable cards): ~2.5× CTR vs standard. Marketing-only.
- Send windows: 9–11 AM India for awareness, 5–7 PM for action. Tue–Thu best. Avoid Mondays and post-9 PM.
- Phone tier ladder: 250 (unverified) → 1K → 10K → 100K → unlimited. Tier upgrade every 6 hours, requires 50%+ capacity + high quality.
- **No Hinglish in templates** — Meta rejects mixed-language. Submit separate pure English + pure Hindi versions. Hinglish only inside free 24-hour windows.
- **No unofficial API / QR linking** — violates Meta ToS, 10M Indian accounts banned/month, 2.6% appeal rate, DPDPA penalties up to ₹250 crore. ZeroPaper does this; we never will.

### Outreach sequence (configurable per wedding)

| Timing | Action | Type |
|---|---|---|
| 12–16 weeks out | Save-the-date (rich media) | Marketing |
| 8–10 weeks | RSVP request with website link | Marketing |
| 7 weeks | RSVP nudge for non-responders | Marketing |
| 6 weeks | Second nudge + escalate persistent non-responders to human | Marketing |
| 5 weeks | Travel detail collection from confirmed guests | Conversation |
| 4 weeks | Transportation / shuttle assignment | Utility |
| 2 weeks | Event schedule reminder | Utility |
| 3 days | Final logistics summary | Utility |
| 1 day | Day-of details | Utility |
| 1 week after | Thank you | Utility |

## 8. Two-Way Operations Channel

The same WhatsApp number serves two audiences with different behaviors:

**Outbound — for guests:**
- Proactive outreach (above)
- 24/7 Concierge for questions
- Utility updates (shuttle assignments, schedule changes, day-of details)

**Inbound — for couple / planners / family:**
- Text & voice commands: *"Uncle Raj cancelled, remove him from the shuttle"*
- Real-time changes: *"Mehendi artist moved to 2pm, update everyone"*
- Voice-message intake → Whisper transcription → parse → execute
- Status queries: *"How many people have confirmed?"* → instant dashboard summary
- Emergency handling without opening the web app

**Routing:** the system identifies whether a sender is in `guests` or `wedding_admins` and routes to different system prompts / capabilities. Admin messages can mutate data; guest messages are read-only.

## 9. Competitive Landscape

We researched the full landscape (7 papers covering WhatsApp API constraints, pricing, competitors, destination logistics, template design, NRI market, and unofficial-API feasibility).

| Competitor | Strength | Gap they leave |

| **Joy** | "Smart RSVP" | No proactive outreach, no India / NRI focus, no logistics service |
| **The Knot** | Vendor matching, US scale | No WhatsApp, no Indian wedding fluency |
| **Zola** | AI task-splitting | Tool seller, not service. No guest comms |
| **WeddingWire** | Vendor chatbots | No guest-facing coordination |
| **Wedflow** | WhatsApp logistics | B2B-only — sells to planners, not couples |
| **Jubilyn** | WhatsApp invitations | RSVP-only |

**Nobody is doing guest logistics work as a B2C WhatsApp-native service.** Nobody uses WhatsApp Flows for in-chat RSVP. Nobody addresses non-Indian guests at Indian weddings. Nobody charges for outcomes. **This gap is the opportunity.**

## 10. Day-of Coordination Partnership Model

Day-of coordinators ($2,500–$4,500) are an established category. They come in 4–6 weeks before the wedding, learn the plan, and run the day on-site.

**The clean split:**
- **Phera** → intelligence work (guest coordination, travel logistics, communication, timeline) for weeks/months leading up.
- **Local day-of coordinator** → judgment + physical work (on-site vendors, crisis handling, ceremony flow, day-of execution).

The couple gets planner-quality outcomes at a fraction of the cost. Phase 3+ we can build a referral network of vetted day-of coordinators in key cities.

## 11. Complementary Positioning

Not every couple needs a website from us. Some already have Zola or Joy. We ask early in onboarding: **"Do you already have a wedding website?"**

- **Yes** → skip website creation, go straight to guest list import + logistics. Phera complements their existing site.
- **No** → build one (DIY, AI-assisted, or expert-guided). The site becomes the digital invitation + RSVP collection point.

This expands TAM beyond "couples who need a wedding website" to "any couple who needs guest coordination" — and positions Phera as additive, not competitive with other platforms.

## 12. DPDPA 2023 Compliance

India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act. Full enforcement May 2027. We build compliance from day one.

- Every first WhatsApp message → explicit opt-in after clear notice.
- Consent offered in **English + Hindi minimum**.
- Data retention → erase guest data when wedding purpose fulfilled or consent withdrawn.
- Breach notification: **72 hours to DPB + affected individuals**.
- Children (<18): verifiable parental consent required.

Penalty exposure for ignoring this is up to ₹250 crore per violation. Compliance is not optional.

## 14. Roadmap (Phased)

### Phase 0 — Validate Demand (no code)

Talk to 10–15 wedding planners and 3–5 recent-Indian-wedding couples. Document the real guest coordination workflow. Goal: that workflow becomes the product spec.

### Phase 1 — Messaging Pivot + Core Service Build *(WE ARE HERE)*

- Landing page rewrite (4 service pillars + "and much more")
- Pricing restructure (no free tier, multi-currency)
- Onboarding adds "Do you already have a website?" branch
- **Build the outreach engine** — WhatsApp templates submitted to Meta, sequencing system on Vercel Cron, per-guest status tracking, response handoff to Concierge AI
- **Build the Control Tower** — replaces the Overview page; response tracker, action queue, outreach timeline, escalation queue
- Admin sidebar reorg into Operations / Wedding Setup / More
- WhatsApp admin command channel (text + voice)
- Guest list import improvements (column mapping, manual wizard, smart paste)

### Phase 2 — First 5–10 Real Weddings (2–3 months)

Run the service for real couples at introductory pricing. KV personally handles all escalations (human in the loop). Learn what breaks. Track time per wedding to refine unit economics.

### Phase 3 — Automate and Expand (after validation)

Multi-language support (build for languages that actually came up), voice AI calling agents for unresponsive guests, Google Contacts integration, voice-enabled Build with AI, Gen Z visual redesign, day-of coordinator referral network, vendor procurement pilot in 2–3 cities.

### Phase 4 — Scale (6–12 months out)

Formalize Planner tier based on planner signals. Expand to non-destination Indian weddings if the model works. Pricing optimization.
