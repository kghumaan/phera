# Phera Monetization Strategy

> **Living document.** Where the money actually is, per service — reconciled against a skeptical financial review and an Indian/NRI cultural-fit review (so the numbers are defensible and not Western copy-paste). Companion to `docs/SERVICES-CATALOG.md` and `docs/PLANNER-JOURNEY.md`.
>
> Last updated: 2026-06-29 · Source: monetization research fan-out (6 web-research agents → profit scoring → skeptic + NRI-culture critics → reconciled roadmap)

---

## Bottom line

**The only money reliably banked today is the one-time Pro plan fee ($349 NRI / ₹9,999 domestic).** Everything else is a *build*, and most "big" wedding-revenue engines are smaller for Phera than the Western benchmarks suggest:

- **Fastest profit-per-effort:** a **free shagun ledger** as a Free→Pro hook, plus high-margin **third-party-funded affiliates (e-visa, travel insurance, hotel, registry) aimed strictly at the 10–30% non-Indian "reverse-destination" guests** — the one place the Western affiliate model genuinely fits.
- **The genuinely large engines — room-block, venue, NRI vendor commission — are real but gated** on IATA/IATAN accreditation *and* on winning the contract-of-record against the family's incumbent planner. Model them **net** of splits/capture (often **$0 near-term**), pursued **through planner partnerships over 12–18 months** — never as base-case revenue.
- **A defensible *mature blended* figure is ~$1,500–$5,000 incremental per high-end NRI wedding** that uses the full managed + commission stack — **not** the $15K+ that stacking best-case headlines implies, and **~$0 incremental for domestic India**.

**What we deliberately do NOT monetize directly** (and why): the website (commodity racing to free — Zola/Joy/Knot give it away); the schedule / RSVP / guest list (the free flywheel — gating it kills the funnel); Western-style *physical* registry for Indian guests (~$0 attach); domestic vendor lead-gen (Indian vendors don't buy leads); flights (~$2 payout — a data feature, not revenue); and **any visible take-rate on cash shagun** (reads as "skimming the blessing" — culturally radioactive).

## Phased roadmap

**Phase 0 (now, wks 0–6) — Lock the free flywheel + wire the Pro SEND trigger.**
Free: schedule + cultural guide + RSVP + guest import + FAQ + photo album. Pro: reminders (dress-code/event/thank-you) + concierge + broadcast. *Impact:* raise free→Pro to a measured 5–15%; each = full $349–599. **This is the near-term P&L — not the commission lines.** Nothing here is contested.

**Phase 1 (wks 4–12) — Turn on high-margin, low-gating, third-party-funded lines.**
Free shagun/Vyavahar ledger (+ optional digital collection) · reverse-destination affiliate funnel (registry/e-visa/hotel/insurance) · multi-event e-invite *design* · livestream resell. *Impact:* honest **$50–250/wedding** on NRI weddings with non-Indian guests; ~$0 domestic. No accreditation, culture-fit, high margin.

**Phase 2 (months 3–6) — Planner channel as commission-partner + managed guest-logistics tier.**
Planner agency accounts (rev-share) · Premium "remote guest-logistics done-for-you" ($499–1,500 NRI-only, net ~$600–1,500) · liaison-managed data collection · vendor coordination + contract vault (trust substrate). Re-anchor managed to **remote** logistics the in-India planner does poorly — not cheap, already-staffed day-of.

**Phase 3 (months 6–18) — Big commission engines, accreditation-gated, through planners.**
NRI vendor-sourcing commission (build first — most enforceable) · room-block · venue accommodation · hampers (flat fee). Model **net** of splits × capture; often $0 until accredited + contract-of-record won. **Upside, never base case.**

## Bundles

1. **The Wedding Website** *(free entry)* — website + FAQ + guest import + RSVP + photo album. Commodity → free top-of-funnel. Only micro-upsell: custom domain (~$15/yr).
2. **Guest Experience** *(free engagement — the moat)* — schedule + cultural guide + dress-code display + photo album + free shagun ledger. Teaches reverse-destination guests; seeds the data flywheel; its SEND actions unlock Pro.
3. **Pro — Guest Communications & Logistics** *(self-serve, $349 / ₹9,999)* — reminders + concierge + broadcast + rooms + shuttle + run-sheet + enrichment. Gate on `isPro`, all features. SEND is the trigger.
4. **Premium — Done-For-You Remote Guest Operations** *(managed, $499–1,500 NRI-only)* — human-escalation concierge + remote RSVP chasing + travel/room wrangling + liaison collection + dietary aggregation + hampers + livestream. Sell the *work*, anchored to remote logistics; complements the family's planner.
5. **Commission & Affiliate Engine** *(couple-invisible, third-party-funded)* — reverse-destination affiliates + e-visa/insurance/hotel + digital shagun + NRI vendor commission + room-block + venue. Tiered by enforceability; lead all cash UX with "no skim" transparency.
6. **Planner Agency** *(B2B multiplier)* — agency accounts (commission-share) + vendor coordination + budget/payment tracking + contract vault. The path to the otherwise-blocked Layer-3 commissions; haircut commission lines by the planner's cut.

## Pro-conversion playbook

1. **Keep the entire acquisition layer free forever** — website, schedule, cultural guide, FAQ, guest import, per-event RSVP, photo album, shagun ledger. Gating any of these kills the funnel.
2. **Build the free schedule WITH dress codes first**, so the couple sees value before any ask. The conversion moment is the verb **SEND**: *"Your schedule's ready — now let Phera message all 300 guests their dress code, answer questions 24/7, and chase RSVPs across time zones."*
3. **Wire RSVP/guest-data signals to surface the Pro prompt at the moment of need:** *"X guests are flying in / need rooms — unlock room assignments + shuttle"*; *"Y haven't replied — let Phera auto-chase them."* Earned, contextual upgrade — never a generic paywall.
4. **Free shagun ledger = second culturally-native hook.** Track cash gifts free (zero skim optics); offer optional digital collection for guests abroad as flat-fee upside.
5. **For reverse-destination guests, proactively offer** "tell me what to buy / do I need a visa / book travel insurance" — the one segment where affiliate converts, monetized without touching the couple's price.
6. **Escalate to managed Premium only on real remote-logistics pain** (300-guest diaspora list across time zones). Sell as a *complement* to the family's planner at $499–1,500 — not a $1,500–3,500 day-of replacement.
7. **Instrument everything:** actual free→Pro rate, per-trigger conversion, WhatsApp template categorization (utility ₹0.115 vs marketing ₹0.86 — 7.5× swing). Concierge's "attributable $70–240" is **internal prioritization only — never added to a revenue total**.
8. **Lean on planner agency accounts** for pre-qualified high-budget NRI weddings (cheapest, highest-intent CAC); treat every engaged guest as a future couple (referral = the only compounding value; count it as CAC reduction, not recurring revenue).

## Open business decisions (founder)

1. **Commission structure & who pays (room-block/venue):** pursue IATA/IATAN accreditation (multi-quarter, capital + legal + attrition liability) to be agent-of-record, *or* ride a HotelPlanner-style rev-share partner (~2.5% to you). Model accommodation as **one** GMV pool. 12–18 mo bet, not a revenue line.
2. **Managed-tier rate & scope:** set Premium ($499–1,500 NRI-only); scope to **remote guest-logistics**, explicitly not physical day-of. Decide whether run-sheet/shuttle/concierge live in Pro **or** managed (not both). Accept the human-labor capacity ceiling.
3. **Registry vs shagun payment model:** demote registry to non-Indian-guest-only; lead with a **free** shagun ledger. Choose collection model: flat enablement fee / processor-passthrough (Hitchd-style) vs nothing — a visible take-rate on cash gifts is culturally radioactive. (Domestic UPI MDR ≈ 0 → no spread anyway.)
4. **Vendor lead pricing — two products:** kill domestic CPL/listing as near-term revenue. Decide NRI commission model: payment rail (escrow, 8–15% target, unvalidated) vs flat "verified NRI lead" fee. The only enforceable vendor monetization.
5. **Planner channel economics:** rev-share vs per-seat. Per-seat = channel conflict with the gatekeeper of the commissions you want; rev-share aligns but dilutes every commission line. Pick one; model commissions net of the planner's cut.
6. **Hampers & "no skim" posture:** offer at all? If so, flat disclosed managed fee, not a hidden 15–25% markup. Resolve the principle: disclosed third-party-funded commissions (hotel/airline) are safe; couple-facing take-rates on gifts/cash are the optics landmine.
7. **Blended financial model:** commit to **one** blended per-wedding model with explicit eligibility × attach × capture multipliers, segmented NRI vs domestic (domestic ≈ $0 for nearly every commission line). Re-state every commission net of Stripe, host-agency/IATA splits, labor, fulfillment. Mature blended target **$1,500–5,000** on high-end NRI weddings using the full stack.
