# Phera — Outreach DM Templates

Reusable IG/X DM templates for founder-led outreach. Match tone, keep personalization light but specific.

## Rule set for every DM

- Lead with intro: `I'm KV, building an AI product for the wedding industry to handle guest logistics (used it at my own wedding this year).`
- One-line compliment referencing a SPECIFIC detail from their post (wedding name, venue, caption line, style note). No generic "beautiful work."
- "Connecting with [planners/brides/vendors] to learn more about the industry and how guest logistics actually gets handled, especially on [relevant context: NRI / destination / intimate / luxury / Indian]."
- Ask: "Open to a quick 15-min call?"
- Close: "Not pitching — genuinely want to learn from someone doing this well."
- No calendly link in first DM. Ask first, send link after yes.
- Send time: Tue-Thu, 10am-12pm local to recipient.

## Template — Planners (PRIMARY)

> Hi [Name/Team] — I'm KV, building an AI product for the wedding industry to handle guest logistics (used it at my own wedding this year).
> Loved [specific detail from their post — wedding name at venue, caption line, style note].
> I'm connecting with planners to learn more about the industry and how guest logistics actually gets handled, especially on [NRI / destination / intimate / luxury / Indian] weddings.
> Open to a quick 15-min call? Not pitching — genuinely want to learn from someone doing this well.

## Template — Vendors (decor / photo / etc. where no planner credited)

> Hi [Name] — I'm KV, building an AI product for the wedding industry to handle guest logistics.
> [Specific compliment on their work].
> Curious — do you also handle any guest coordination side or strictly [decor/photo]? Would love 15 min either way to learn how the coordination side gets handled on weddings like this. Happy to show what I built if useful.

## Targeting — Planners

- IG hashtags: #indianweddingplanner #nriwedding #destinationindianwedding #indianweddingsinusa #southasianwedding #desiweddingplanner #[city]weddingplanner
- Filter: active (posted last 30 days), real weddings (not styled shoots only), tagged by couples, team of 2-8
- Priority: US-based NRI planners > India-based destination planners > UK/Canada/Dubai
- Skip: <1K followers + <5 weddings, 100K+ agency, decor-only accounts

## Targeting — DIY Brides / Couples

- IG hashtags: #bridetobe2026 #bridetobe2027 #indianbride #mywedding #weddingplanning #diybride #indianweddingplanning #dulhan2026
- Signals for DIY (no planner hired):
  - Bio shows wedding countdown ("Getting married Dec 2026") but no planner tagged
  - Posts show couple coordinating vendors themselves (multiple individual vendor tags, no agency umbrella)
  - Complains about guest coordination / RSVPs / follow-ups in captions or stories
  - Engagement post but no planner announcement
  - "My wedding journey" / "wedding diary" style content
- Also good: r/IndianWeddings, r/ABCDesis, FB groups ("Indian Wedding Planning 2026") — higher DIY density than IG
- Skip: influencers (they want sponsorship, not tools), celebrity couples, accounts that already tag a planner

## Template — DIY Bride / Couple (DIFFERENT TONE — peer-to-peer, not founder-learning)

> Hi [Name]! Congrats on the engagement/upcoming wedding! I just had my own this year (150 guests, still recovering) and ended up building an AI tool to handle the guest logistics — RSVPs, travel, shuttles, answering all the "what's the dress code" Qs from aunties 24/7. Saw you're planning yours now — would love to show you what I built if it'd save you some sanity. Free beta access if helpful. No pressure either way!

**Why different tone for brides:**
- Peer voice ("just had my own") not founder voice ("building for the industry")
- Lead with outcome that bride relates to (RSVPs + aunties), not features
- Free beta = tangible offer, lowers ask friction
- Shorter — brides get spammy DMs constantly, cut to value fast

## Tracking

Keep spreadsheet per batch:
- Handle | Name | City | Specialty | Last post | Sent date | Reply Y/N | Call booked Y/N | Notes
- Cap DM volume: 10/day max from personal account
- Follow up once after 5 days, then drop
- Review reply rate per 50 DMs — if <15%, rewrite template

## Tone guardrails

DO:
- Specific detail from their post
- Short (4 sentences max for planners, 3 for brides)
- "Not pitching" / "not selling" unlock line
- "Happy to show" not "let me show" (pull not push)

DON'T:
- Calendly/scheduling link in first DM
- Generic "love your work" compliments
- Multi-paragraph story (save for call)
- Attach waitlist form / signup link
- Use "revolutionary" / "game-changing" / buzzwords
- Emoji-heavy (1-2 max if any)
